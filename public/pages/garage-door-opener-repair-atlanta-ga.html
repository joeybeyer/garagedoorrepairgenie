Which Repair Fits This Problem?

If the door moves smoothly by hand but fails from the wall button, choose opener repair. If the door is heavy by hand, choose spring or cable diagnosis before replacing the motor. The trade-off is cost control: a sensor or gear repair can be cheaper than a new opener, but a strained opener should not mask a counterbalance problem.

Atlanta calls split between older intown doors around Midtown and Grant Park and larger suburban doors around Buckhead, so dispatch has to separate sensor issues from true counterbalance failures.

Force settings matter when heat, cold, or track drag changes resistance. Sensor alignment matters when the door starts down and reverses before touching the floor.

Simple remote, keypad, and sensor fixes can take minutes; gear, rail, or logic-board diagnosis often takes 30 to 60 minutes before the repair path is clear.
