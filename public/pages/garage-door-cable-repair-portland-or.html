Garage Door Cable Repair Near Me in Portland

Lift cables work with the springs to raise the door evenly. When one cable frays, snaps, or jumps the drum, the door can wedge in the track and bend panels if the opener keeps running. If you are in Portland, OR, call the Genie and describe the symptom. We route the call around the actual problem, not a generic appointment script.

Safety warning: Do not force a garage door with a broken spring, snapped cable, or off-track section. Garage doors are heavy and can be dangerous when the counterbalance system fails.

Which Repair Fits This Problem?

If a cable hangs loose or the door dropped crooked, choose cable repair. If both cables look tight but the door is heavy, ask for spring diagnosis instead. The trade-off is pairing: replacing both lift cables usually costs more than one cable, but it keeps the door lifting evenly.

Portland service calls often cluster around wet-season noise and sensor trouble because tracked-in grit and damp rollers make small alignment problems show up as full door failures.

Cable diameter and drum seating matter when the door lifts unevenly. Pair replacement matters when one cable snapped because the other cable usually carried the same cycle load.

Cable repairs are normally quoted as a pair because both sides carry the same door weight. Replacing one cable can leave the older side as the next failure point.
