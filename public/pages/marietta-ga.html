Why Marietta Doors Fail Now

  • 1990s-2000s housing stock means a lot of original springs are at end-of-life right now.
  • Hot summers stress openers — force settings drift in extreme heat.
  • Pollen season is hard on photo-eye sensors — spring and fall sees a wave of "door won't close" calls.
  • Larger 3-car garages are common, so multi-door tune-ups are routine.
  • Pine-tree limb falls during storms cause off-track and panel-damage calls every year.

Marietta Multi-Door Homes: A Note on Scheduling

If you have a 2- or 3-car garage with multiple doors, schedule the service call so all the doors can be looked at at the same time. Two reasons:

  • Doors of the same age tend to fail in clusters — if one spring just broke, the others are statistically likely to fail soon.
  • Multi-door tune-ups typically come with a small bundled discount over individual service calls. Worth asking the dispatcher.

Same applies to opener replacement: if you're upgrading one to a smart belt-drive, doing all of them at once typically saves on the trip charge.

Marietta Garage Door Questions

How fast is dispatch into East Cobb / West Cobb?

Same-day for calls placed before noon in most cases. Sandy Plains, Powers Park, and Marietta Square are typically the fastest. West Cobb past Lost Mountain can take a bit longer due to distance.

My door is from 1998 and the spring just broke. Should I replace the whole door?

Not based on age alone. A 1998 door with sound panels, intact tracks, and minor cosmetic wear can run another 10-15 years easily with new springs and cables. Replace the door if the panels are bent or rusted through, the tracks are bent, or you want a major aesthetic change — otherwise repair is much cheaper.

Are pollen-related sensor problems really a thing?

Yes — especially in early April when the yellow pine pollen blankets everything in metro Atlanta. The pollen layer on the sensor lens breaks the beam and the door won't close. Wipe the sensors monthly during pollen season; it's a 30-second fix that prevents service calls.

Should I consider a battery backup opener?

Worth it if you've ever been stuck during a power outage, which happens 1-3 times a year in most of metro Atlanta. The upcharge for a battery-backup unit is usually $50-$100 over a standard opener at install time. Retrofit batteries are also available for some existing models.

Do you handle Smyrna and Vinings too?

Yes — most of central Cobb County is covered through the Marietta dispatch network. Mention your specific neighborhood when you call so the dispatcher can route to the closest tech.
