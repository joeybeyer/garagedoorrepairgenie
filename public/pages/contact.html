What Happens After You Contact Us

  1. Dispatch reviews your details. Phone calls get a real-time response; form submissions get a callback within 1 business hour during business days.
  2. You get a real ETA. Same-day for most calls placed before noon. Emergency calls jump the queue.
  3. The tech arrives, diagnoses on-site, and writes a flat-rate quote before any work starts.
  4. You decide. If you go forward, the service-call fee usually waives. If you don't, you owe just the dispatch fee.
  5. Repair is completed and the door tested for balance, force, and safety reverse before the tech leaves.
