Why Roswell Storm Damage Doors Are Tricky

Heavy tree cover means a big windstorm or ice event tends to drop limbs on garage roofs and the front of garage doors specifically. The damage profile is different from typical wear:

  • Panels may be dented but still functional — cosmetic only, can wait.
  • Tracks may be subtly bent — door operates roughly, slowly worsening if ignored.
  • Cables can be shock-loaded by an impact even if the door looks fine — failure may come weeks later.
  • Opener gear can be stripped from an attempted lift against an obstruction — sometimes immediate, sometimes a slow drop in performance.

After any limb-fall or impact event, a 30-minute inspection is a good investment even if the door still cycles. The tech can identify hidden damage before it becomes an emergency.

Roswell Garage Door Questions

How fast is dispatch into Roswell?

Same-day in most cases for calls placed before noon. Historic Roswell and East Roswell are typically fastest. Outer Crabapple and Mountain Park can take a bit longer. Horseshoe Bend traffic on Holcomb Bridge sometimes slows afternoon dispatch.

I have a 3-car garage with three doors — can you service all of them in one visit?

Yes — this is actually the most efficient way to do it. The tech is already on-site, the trip charge is fixed, and any add-ons (lubrication, balance check, sensor wipe) cost very little incremental labor when bundled. Multi-door tune-ups typically come with a small bundled discount.

A tree limb hit my garage door last week and it still works — do I really need a tech?

Yes, ideally. The door may have absorbed shock damage that's not visible from outside — bent tracks, stressed cables, or a hairline panel crack. Catching it before it fails is much cheaper than catching it after. A 30-minute inspection is the right move.

Are heavy insulated doors worth the upcharge in Roswell?

Yes if your garage is attached and heated/cooled. The R-value of an insulated door meaningfully reduces conditioned-air leakage. If your garage is unheated and detached, insulated doors mostly just buy you noise reduction — nice but not essential.

Do you do work near the Roswell Square historic district?

Yes — functional repairs (springs, cables, opener, hardware) on historic-area homes are routine. Replacement of historic-style panels sometimes needs custom sourcing; factor in 1-2 weeks for that. Functional repairs go on the first visit.
