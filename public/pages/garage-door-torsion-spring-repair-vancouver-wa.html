Garage Door Torsion Spring Broken in Vancouver

Torsion springs are high-tension parts mounted on the bar above the door. The correct repair means matching spring size to door weight, replacing weak paired springs when needed, and rebalancing the door before the opener is used again. If you are in Vancouver, WA, call the Genie and describe the symptom. We route the call around the actual problem, not a generic appointment script.

Safety warning: Do not force a garage door with a broken spring, snapped cable, or off-track section. Garage doors are heavy and can be dangerous when the counterbalance system fails.
