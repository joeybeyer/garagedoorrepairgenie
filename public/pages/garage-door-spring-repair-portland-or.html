Fix Garage Door Spring in Portland

Garage door springs carry the door's weight. When one fails, the opener is no longer doing normal work; it is trying to lift a heavy, unbalanced door. If you are in Portland, OR, call the Genie and describe the symptom. We route the call around the actual problem, not a generic appointment script.

Safety warning: Do not force a garage door with a broken spring, snapped cable, or off-track section. Garage doors are heavy and can be dangerous when the counterbalance system fails.

Garage Spring Repair

These are the signs that point to a real service call instead of a quick reset:

  • You heard a loud bang from the garage.
  • The opener runs but the door barely moves.
  • The door feels extremely heavy by hand.
  • There is a visible gap in the spring above the door.
