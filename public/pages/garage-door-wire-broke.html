Problem and Solution

Homeowners often call a broken lift cable a wire. Either way, stop using the door and get it checked.

Call the Genie. A local provider can review the symptoms, explain the likely service category, and help schedule repair where available.

Signs You Need Service

  • Loose wire near the track
  • Door is tilted
  • Cable is hanging down
  • Spring or cable noise happened first
Safety warning: Do not force a garage door with a broken spring, snapped cable, or off-track section. Garage doors are heavy and can be dangerous when the counterbalance system fails.
